Connect your Alpaca paper account

StratTraderPro routes your strategy's orders to a broker. In this release the broker is Alpaca, in paper (simulated) trading mode. You connect Alpaca by generating your own API keys on Alpaca's dashboard and pasting them into StratTraderPro — we never take custody of your funds, and your keys are encrypted at rest.

Paper trading is free. You don't need to fund an account or wait for approval to trade paper. Every order in this release is simulated against Alpaca's paper endpoint https://paper-api.alpaca.markets — no real money moves.

1. Create a free Alpaca account

  1. Go to alpaca.markets and sign up. It's free.
  2. Verify your email and log in to the Alpaca dashboard.

2. Generate your PAPER API keys

  1. In the Alpaca dashboard, switch to Paper Trading using the account toggle. Make sure you are in the Paper view, not Live.
  2. Open the API Keys panel under the paper account and click Generate (or Regenerate).
  3. Alpaca shows two values: an API Key ID and a Secret Key. Copy both now.
The Secret Key is shown once. Copy it before you close the dialog. And note: regenerating your keys invalidates the previous pair. If you regenerate later, the keys you pasted into StratTraderPro stop working and your broker status flips to Error — you'll need to come back here and paste the new pair.

3. Paste the keys into StratTraderPro

  1. In StratTraderPro, go to Settings → Brokers (/settings/brokers).
  2. Click Connect Alpaca Paper.
  3. Paste your API Key ID and Secret Key into the two fields and save.

Your Secret Key is write-only — once saved, it is never shown back to you or sent to your browser again. It's stored encrypted with the same key-management we use for your two-factor and webhook secrets.

4. Test the connection

Click Test connection. StratTraderPro asks Alpaca for your account details and shows your paper account number and buying power. When that appears, you're connected and the broker shows a Connected badge.

Errors you might see

MessageWhat it means
BROKER_AUTH_FAILED Alpaca rejected the keys. Usually a typo, or you regenerated the keys in Alpaca after pasting them here. Regenerate and re-paste both values.
BROKER_LIVE_KEYS_FORBIDDEN You pasted live keys. This release is paper-only. Generate keys from Alpaca's Paper section instead.

Good to know

  • US stocks & ETFs only in this release. Options, crypto, and futures alerts are rejected.
  • If your broker badge turns to Error later, the most common cause is regenerating your Alpaca keys — just paste the new pair.
  • Removing a broker asks for your two-factor code and stops order routing for that account.
