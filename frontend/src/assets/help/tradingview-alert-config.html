Configure your TradingView alert

Once your strategy is uploaded (or once you've enabled a system strategy), open Configure webhook to get the URL and HMAC secret you'll need on TradingView's side.

1. Get your URL + secret

  1. From Strategies, click Configure webhook next to the strategy you want to fire.
  2. The modal shows your unique URL like https://api.strattraderpro.com/hooks/v1/<your-uuid>/<strategy-uuid>/.
  3. The HMAC secret is shown once, with a yellow border. Copy it immediately.
Lost the secret? Click Rotate secret. This generates a new secret and invalidates the old one — any existing TradingView alerts using the previous secret will stop working until you update them with the new secret.

2. Copy the TradingView template

The Copy TradingView alert template button copies a JSON block to your clipboard with TradingView placeholders pre-filled and your sig set to the just-revealed secret:

{
  "strategy": "{{strategy}}",
  "action": "{{strategy.order.action}}",
  "symbol": "{{ticker}}",
  "qty": "{{strategy.order.contracts}}",
  "order_type": "MKT",
  "sig": "your-secret-here",
  "idempotency_key": "{{strategy.order.id}}-{{time}}"
}

3. Configure the alert in TradingView

  1. Open the chart for your symbol.
  2. Add your Pine strategy script to the chart.
  3. Click the Alerts button → Create alert.
  4. In the Condition dropdown, pick the strategy and the signal you want to fire.
  5. Open the Notifications tab → enable Webhook URL → paste your StratTraderPro URL.
  6. Open the Message tab → paste the JSON template you copied from StratTraderPro. Adjust any fields as needed.
  7. Save.

4. Test it

  • From the Webhook modal, the Test against schema button validates your payload template (with current placeholder values) against your saved JSON Schema. It does not place any orders.
  • Once you're confident the JSON is shaped correctly, fire a test alert from TradingView. The order pipeline lands in M04 — until then, the webhook ingest endpoint is not live and alerts will be rejected at the URL.

Troubleshooting

  • Webhook doesn't fire from TradingView at all. TradingView may have disabled webhooks for your account tier — check your subscription. Webhooks require Pro+.
  • Alert fires but StratTraderPro rejects it. The sig field must match your current secret exactly. If you've rotated, update the alert.
