Configure your TradingView alert

Once your strategy is uploaded (or once you've enabled a system strategy), open Configure webhook to get the URL and webhook secret you'll need on TradingView's side. The webhook endpoint is now live — a correctly-configured alert places a real (paper) order through your connected Alpaca account.

Before you start: connect an Alpaca paper account under Settings → Brokers first (see Connect your Alpaca paper account). Without a connected broker, an alert is accepted at the URL but rejected downstream with NO_BROKER_CONNECTED and no order is placed.

1. Get your URL + secret

  1. From Strategies, click Configure webhook next to the strategy you want to fire.
  2. The modal shows your unique URL like https://api.strattraderpro.com/hooks/v1/<your-uuid>/<strategy-uuid>/.
  3. The webhook secret is shown once, with a yellow border. Copy it immediately. StratTraderPro puts this secret in the alert's sig field and checks that inbound alerts carry the matching value — think of it as a password on your webhook URL.
Lost the secret? Click Rotate secret. This generates a new secret and invalidates the old one — any existing TradingView alerts using the previous secret will stop working until you update them with the new secret.

2. Copy the TradingView template

The Copy TradingView alert template button copies a JSON block to your clipboard with TradingView placeholders pre-filled and your sig set to the just-revealed secret:

{
  "strategy": "{{strategy}}",
  "action": "{{strategy.order.action}}",
  "symbol": "{{ticker}}",
  "qty": "{{strategy.order.contracts}}",
  "order_type": "MKT",
  "sig": "your-secret-here",
  "idempotency_key": "{{strategy.order.id}}-{{time}}"
}

3. Configure the alert in TradingView

  1. Open the chart for your symbol.
  2. Add your Pine strategy script to the chart.
  3. Click the Alerts button → Create alert.
  4. In the Condition dropdown, pick the strategy and the signal you want to fire.
  5. Open the Notifications tab → enable Webhook URL → paste your StratTraderPro URL.
  6. Open the Message tab → paste the JSON template you copied from StratTraderPro. Adjust any fields as needed.
  7. Save.

4. Test it

  • From the Webhook modal, the Test against schema button validates your payload template (with current placeholder values) against your saved JSON Schema. It does not place any orders.
  • Once you're confident the JSON is shaped correctly, fire a live alert from TradingView. The endpoint POST /hooks/v1/<your-uuid>/<strategy-uuid>/ is live: a valid alert is queued and an order is placed on your connected Alpaca paper account. Watch your Dashboard — the order and its fill appear within a few seconds.
Fire during US market hours. Market orders sent when the US market is closed queue at the broker and don't fill until the next open — so you won't see an immediate fill on your dashboard. Test with a market order during regular US trading hours (9:30 AM–4:00 PM Eastern), or use a limit order.

Troubleshooting

  • Webhook doesn't fire from TradingView at all. TradingView may have disabled webhooks for your account tier — check your subscription. Webhooks require Pro+.
  • Alert fires but StratTraderPro rejects it. The sig field must match your current secret exactly. If you've rotated, update the alert.
  • Alert accepted (200) but no order shows up. Usually NO_BROKER_CONNECTED (connect an Alpaca paper account first), ORDER_UNSUPPORTED_ASSET (this release supports US stocks and ETFs only), or the market is closed so a market order is queued. Check the strategy's activity/orders view for the reason.
