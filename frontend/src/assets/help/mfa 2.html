Setting up two-factor authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication protects your StratTraderPro account by requiring a code from your phone in addition to your password every time you sign in. Trades, broker connections, and risk settings are locked behind 2FA, so enabling it is required before going live.

What you need

  • A TOTP-compatible authenticator app on your phone: Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, or Bitwarden.
  • A safe place to store 10 backup codes — a password manager, printed sheet, or sealed envelope.

Step-by-step

  1. Sign in and open Settings → Security.
  2. Click Set up 2FA.
  3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or copy the secret string and add it manually.
  4. Type the 6-digit code your app shows. The code rotates every 30 seconds — if it expires, just type the next one.
  5. Save the 10 backup codes that appear. They will not be shown again. Each works exactly once.
  6. Done — next time you sign in, you'll be asked for a code after your password.
Tip — clock skew. 2FA codes are tied to your phone's clock. We tolerate a 30-second drift, but if your phone's clock is way off you'll see "code didn't match". On Android, enable Settings → System → Date & time → Set automatically.

What if I lose my phone?

  1. On the sign-in MFA screen, click "Use a backup code instead" and enter one of your 10 saved codes.
  2. Once signed in, go to Settings → Security and regenerate your backup codes (the old set is now invalid because you used one).
  3. Then disable and re-enroll 2FA on your new phone.
If you lost both your phone and your backup codes, contact support. We can disable 2FA on your account after a manual identity check. The recovery process is documented in our internal runbook docs/runbooks/user-lost-mfa.md.

Why do you require 2FA for broker actions?

A stolen session cookie or refresh token would let an attacker view your dashboard, but cannot place trades, change risk caps, or alter strategies without a fresh code from your authenticator. This means any compromise is contained — your money is safe even if your browser session is hijacked.
