Connect your TradeStation account

Coming soon. TradeStation support is built into StratTraderPro but is not switched on yet — TradeStation requires us to be granted API access before we can enable it. Until then, Connect TradeStation is hidden or disabled on the Brokers page, and your strategies route through Alpaca. This page explains how connecting TradeStation will work once it's available, so nothing is a surprise on the day.

Unlike Alpaca — where you paste your own API keys — TradeStation uses a "Connect with TradeStation" consent flow. You log in on TradeStation's own site and approve StratTraderPro; we never see or store your TradeStation password. We only receive a secure token that lets us place your strategy's orders, and that token is encrypted at rest.

Paper (simulated) trading. As with Alpaca in this release, TradeStation will connect in simulated mode — no real money moves. Live trading stays switched off across the whole app until a later release.

How connecting will work

  1. Go to Settings → Brokers (/settings/brokers) and click Connect TradeStation.
  2. Your browser is sent to TradeStation's own login and consent page. Sign in there with your TradeStation credentials.
  3. TradeStation asks whether you allow StratTraderPro to view your accounts and place orders. Click Allow.
  4. You're sent back to StratTraderPro. Your TradeStation account(s) appear on the Brokers page with a Connected badge — no keys to copy or paste.

Because you approve access on TradeStation's site and we only keep an encrypted token, you can revoke StratTraderPro at any time from TradeStation's own connected-apps settings, and removing the broker here stops order routing for that account.

Staying connected

The access we're granted refreshes itself quietly in the background, so you won't be asked to log in again for a long time (well beyond a normal trading routine). You only ever need to reconnect if you (or TradeStation) explicitly revoke access.

If you see "Reconnect TradeStation". This means the permission you granted was withdrawn or expired — for example, you revoked StratTraderPro in TradeStation's settings, or it timed out from long disuse. Just click Reconnect TradeStation on the Brokers page and approve access again. Your Alpaca account is unaffected — orders keep routing to Alpaca the whole time.

What you'll be able to trade

  • Stocks & ETFs, same as Alpaca.
  • Options, by their standard OCC option symbol.
  • Futures — TradeStation only. (Alpaca has no futures, so a futures alert sent to an Alpaca account is rejected with ORDER_UNSUPPORTED_ASSET.)

Once both brokers are connected, you can pick which one a strategy routes to, and even run the same alert into different brokers for different accounts — see Reading the Orders page to follow where each order went.

Errors you might see

MessageWhat it means
TradeStation is not enabled / BROKER_DISABLED The feature isn't switched on yet in your release. Nothing to do — it'll appear once we've been granted access.
Reconnect TradeStation / BROKER_REAUTH_REQUIRED The access you granted was revoked or expired. Click Reconnect TradeStation and approve again. Alpaca is unaffected.
Couldn't complete the connection (returned to Brokers with an error) The consent step didn't finish — for example the approval expired, or you closed the TradeStation window partway. Just click Connect TradeStation and try again.
