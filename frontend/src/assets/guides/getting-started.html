Getting started, end to end

This is the whole path from a fresh account to a paper fill you can see on the dashboard. Each step links to a guide with the detail. Expect about twenty minutes, most of it waiting on Alpaca and TradingView rather than on StratTraderPro.

Paper first. Everything below runs against a paper broker account. Nothing here can spend real money. Live trading is a separate, explicitly-gated step and is not covered by this guide.

Where things live

Every page in this guide is reachable from the header. Guides is the tab you are reading now; it is always one click away from any screen.

On the right of the header is the realtime stream indicator. Live means positions and fills are pushed to your browser as they happen; Offline means the page still loads data when you open or refresh it, but nothing updates on its own. It is not an error state and it does not stop orders from being placed.

Step 1 — Verify your email

  1. Register, then open the verification link we email you.
  2. If it never arrives, use Resend verification on the login page.

Until your address is verified you can sign in, but the trading endpoints will refuse you.

Step 2 — Turn on two-factor authentication

This is not optional. Every strategy, broker, order and risk endpoint is behind an MFA gate: without an enrolled authenticator you will see Two-factor authentication required on the dashboard and every panel will be empty.

  1. Go to Settings → Security → Set up two-factor authentication.
  2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app and enter the six-digit code.
  3. Save the ten backup codes. They are shown once. Losing both your authenticator and these codes means losing the account.

Detail: Two-factor authentication.

Step 3 — Set your timezone

Every timestamp in the app — fills, orders, audit entries, backtest windows — is rendered in the timezone on your profile. Set it before you start reading timestamps, not after.

Detail: Profile settings.

Step 4 — Connect a paper broker

  1. Create a paper account at Alpaca and generate paper API keys.
  2. Paste them into Settings → Brokers.
  3. Wait for the connection chip to read Connected.

Without a connected broker an alert is still accepted at the webhook URL, but it is rejected downstream with NO_BROKER_CONNECTED and no order is placed.

Detail: Connect your Alpaca paper account.

Step 5 — Pick or upload a strategy

Open Strategies. System strategies are ready to use. To bring your own Pine script, use Upload strategy — it takes a three-file bundle.

Click a strategy's name (or View) to read its full description before you enable it. How to use opens the step-by-step for that strategy.

Detail: Run a strategy end to end and Upload your first strategy.

Step 6 — Wire the TradingView alert

  1. Configure webhook gives you a URL and a secret. The secret is shown once — copy it now.
  2. In TradingView, create an alert on the same script, paste the URL into Webhook URL, and paste the payload template into Message.
  3. Replace the placeholder in the template's sig field with your secret.

Detail: The webhook payload template and Configure your TradingView alert.

Step 7 — Enable the strategy and watch for the fill

Flip the Enabled toggle. This arms live execution for that strategy, so the app asks you to confirm. From then on, a delivered alert becomes a paper order.

The first fill shows up on the Dashboard under Today's fills, and in full on the Orders page. If nothing arrives, the Orders page is where you find out why — a rejected order carries its reason.
