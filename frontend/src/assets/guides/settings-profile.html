Profile settings: timezone, language, email

Your profile controls three things: the timezone every timestamp in the app is rendered in, the interface language, and whether we email you about account events. Open it at Settings → Profile.

Why the timezone matters

Fill times, order timestamps, audit-log entries, backtest windows and the "generated at" line on admin pages are all rendered in your profile timezone. Leaving it on the default means every timestamp you read is shifted, which is a genuinely bad way to reason about intraday trading.

The timezone is stored as an IANA name (Asia/Jerusalem, America/New_York), not as a fixed UTC offset, so daylight-saving transitions are handled for you.

Setting it, step by step

  1. The line marked Current names the timezone that is saved right now, with its UTC offset in brackets. The same entry is highlighted in the list below and scrolled into view.
  2. To change it, either:
    • type a city or region into Search timezones… — the list filters as you type, so jeru, new_y or london all work; or
    • click Use my browser timezone to take whatever your operating system reports (the name is shown in the link, so you can see what you are about to get).
  3. Click the timezone you want in the list. The Current line updates immediately.
  4. Press Save. A green confirmation appears above the form.
Nothing is saved until you press Save. Selecting a row in the list only stages the change — navigating away discards it.

The count under the list

Showing N of M timezones tells you whether a filter is still applied. If you search and then cannot find your zone, check that line first: a stale search term is the usual reason a zone appears to be "missing".

Language

English only for now. The selector exists so the setting is already on your profile when more languages land; changing it has no visible effect today.

Email notifications

Email me about important account events covers security and account mail — new sign-ins, password and 2FA changes, and account-lifecycle notices. It does not control trade notifications.

Turning it off does not silence transactional email you have to receive, such as email verification and password reset. Those are sent regardless, because without them you cannot use the account.

Display name

The display name is what appears in the header's user menu and beside your entries in the audit log. It is not your login — you always sign in with your email address.
