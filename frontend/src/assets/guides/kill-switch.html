Using the kill switch

The kill switch is your emergency brake. It lets you stop trading and get out of the market fast — either everything at once, or just one strategy. There's also an automatic version that watches your daily losses and steps in for you.

The big red "Halt my trading" button

On your dashboard, the big red Halt my trading button is the one to reach for when you want out now. When you confirm it:

  • we flatten every position across all your connected brokers — sending the orders to close them out;
  • we reject any new alerts for your account until you turn the halt back off;
  • a banner appears across the top of your dashboard so it's obvious trading is halted.
You'll be asked for your MFA code. Because halting your trading is a serious action, we ask for a fresh code from your authenticator app first — the same one you use to sign in. This makes sure it's really you, even if someone else got hold of your browser session. Have your phone handy.

Flattening is fast — the whole thing normally finishes in a few seconds. When you're ready to trade again, turn the halt off from the same button or the Risk page, and new alerts start flowing again. (Positions you closed stay closed; the kill switch doesn't re-open them.)

Per-strategy toggle

Sometimes you only want to pause one strategy, not everything. On the Strategies page each strategy has its own kill-switch toggle:

  • Toggle it on and new alerts for that strategy are turned away — your other strategies keep running normally.
  • No MFA code is needed for a single-strategy toggle; it's a lighter-weight control.
  • Toggle it back off to resume that strategy.
Use the per-strategy toggle when one strategy is misbehaving but the rest of your book is fine. Use the big red button when you want out of everything.

The automatic daily-loss halt

You don't have to be watching. If your losses for the day reach the daily loss limit you set in your risk profile (a dollar amount or a percentage), StratTraderPro trips the halt for you: it flattens your positions and stops new trades — exactly like pressing the big red button.

The daily-loss halt locks until tomorrow. Once it trips, you can't turn it back on yourself for the rest of that trading day — that's the point of a circuit breaker. It releases automatically at the start of the next trading day, and you're back to normal.

To avoid tripping on a momentary bad price quote, we require the loss to be confirmed on two checks in a row (about 30 seconds apart) before we act — a single odd reading won't set it off. When it does trip, you'll see a Daily loss breach entry in your Risk events feed with the loss we measured.

There's also a platform-wide halt that our operators can use in a system-wide emergency — if that's ever active you'll see a banner telling you trading is paused for everyone. It's rare and not something you control.

Seeing what happened

Every halt, release, flatten, and daily-loss trip is recorded in the Risk events feed on the Risk page, with a timestamp and the details — so you can always see exactly when trading was stopped, why, and what got flattened.
