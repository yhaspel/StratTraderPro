## 1. Who we are and what this covers

StratTraderPro ("we", "the platform") is a **paper-trading** strategy-automation
service: you connect a broker's **paper (simulated-money) account**, define
webhook-driven strategies, and we route simulated orders and record the results.
**No real money is ever traded.** Live trading is disabled platform-wide
(`ENABLE_LIVE_TRADING=false`) and is out of scope for this release.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, the legal bases we rely
on, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.
It applies to the API (`api.strattraderpro.com`) and the web app
(`app.strattraderpro.com`).

## 2. Personal data we collect

We collect only what the service needs to function. We do **not** buy personal
data from third parties or sell it to anyone.

## 4. Third parties and subprocessors

We share personal data only with the infrastructure providers we need to run the
service. Each acts as a **processor** on our behalf under a data-processing
agreement, and each receives only the data required for its function.

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Data it can see |
|---|---|---|
| **Alpaca** | Broker for simulated (paper) order execution | Your **paper** broker API keys and the orders/fills routed through them |
| **Resend** | Transactional email delivery | Your email address and the message contents (e.g. an export download link) |
| **Sentry** | Error and exception tracking | Diagnostic error data; may incidentally include your user ID or IP. Request bodies and known secret keys are scrubbed before send |
| **Railway** | Application and database hosting | All data at rest, as the hosting environment |
| **Cloudflare** | TLS termination, CDN, and WAF in front of the app | Request metadata (IP, headers) in transit |

We do not otherwise disclose personal data except where required by law, or to
protect the rights, safety, or security of the platform and its users.

## 5. Data retention

- **Active accounts:** we keep your personal data for as long as your account is
  open.
- **Audit log:** the `audit_log` table is **append-only and hash-chained** — rows
  cannot be edited or deleted (a database trigger forbids it), because the chain's
  integrity is what makes it tamper-evident. Audit rows persist for the life of
  the platform; on account deletion they are **anonymized in place** (see below),
  not removed.
- **Account deletion (30-day soft delete):** when you request deletion we set
  `pending_delete_at = now + 30 days` and email you a confirmation. During that
  window the request is fully reversible (see §6). On expiry, a nightly job
  **anonymizes your account in place**: your `User` row is kept under its primary
  key so audit foreign keys still resolve, but its personal fields (email, name,
  and any linked identifiers) are scrubbed, and your MFA secret and broker keys
  are removed. What remains is an **anonymized audit stub** — enough to preserve
  the audit chain's integrity, with no personal data attached. We never hard-delete
  the row and never delete audit rows.
- **Backups:** encrypted database backups are retained on a rolling schedule for
  disaster recovery and expire automatically; anonymization propagates as old
  backups age out.

## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and comparable laws such as the CCPA) you have the following
rights. To exercise any of them, use the in-app controls below or contact us
(§12).

- **Access / portability — data export.** Call
  **`GET /api/v1/users/me/export/`** (also available from account settings). We
  generate a ZIP archive of your personal data — profile, strategies, orders,
  fills, backtests, and **your own** audit-log events — and email you a download
  link via a signed URL that expires in **24 hours**.
  **Redaction is enforced:** the export **never** contains your broker API keys or
  your MFA secret. Those values are excluded from the archive entirely — not
  masked, not encrypted-and-included, but omitted.
- **Erasure — account deletion.** Call **`POST /api/v1/users/me/delete/`** to
  schedule the 30-day soft delete described in §5. You can cancel any time within
  the window via **`POST /api/v1/users/me/delete/cancel/`**. On expiry your account
  is anonymized in place. Note that the anonymized audit stub is retained by design
  and by legal necessity — it holds no personal data.
- **Rectification.** Correct inaccurate profile data through account settings.
- **Withdraw consent.** Where we rely on consent (Google sign-in), you may
  disconnect it and use email/password instead.
- **Objection / restriction.** You may object to processing based on legitimate
  interest; contact us and we will assess the request.
- **Complaint.** You may lodge a complaint with your local data-protection
  authority.

We respond to rights requests without undue delay and within the statutory time
limits.

## 7. Security measures

- **Passwords** are hashed with **Argon2id** and never stored or logged in
  plaintext.
- **Secrets at rest** — MFA TOTP secrets, broker API keys, and per-strategy webhook
  `sig` secrets — are **Fernet-encrypted** with a key held only in the runtime
  environment, never in source or backups-as-plaintext.
- **In transit** everything is served over **TLS**, with **HSTS** enforced.
- **Multi-factor authentication (TOTP)** is available and enforced on sensitive and
  administrative paths.
- **Audit trail:** security-relevant actions are recorded in the append-only,
  hash-chained audit log, and error-reporting payloads are scrubbed of known
  sensitive keys before leaving the platform.

No system is perfectly secure, but access to your simulated broker account carries
no real-money risk, and live trading is disabled platform-wide.

## 8. Cookies and local storage

We keep the site lightweight. Authentication uses **JSON Web Tokens (JWT)**: a
short-lived access token (~15 minutes) and a longer-lived refresh token (~30 days),
stored by the web app in your browser's storage to keep you signed in. These are
**strictly necessary** for the service to function — they are not analytics or
advertising cookies, and we do not use third-party tracking cookies.

## 9. International data transfers

Our infrastructure providers (§4) may process data in regions outside your own,
including the United States. Where personal data is transferred out of the

**Standard Contractual Clauses** — as the safeguard for that transfer.
