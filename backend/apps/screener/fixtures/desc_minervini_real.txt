[b]Minervini Trend Template[/b]

A long-only trend-following system for [i]liquid large caps[/i]. It wants names
already in a confirmed uptrend, trading near their highs.

[list]
[*]Entry on a breakout above the pivot
[*]Exit on a close below the 50-day
[/list]

[screen]
# Minervini-style trend template, large caps
market_cap: >= 2B
price: 10..1000
volume: >= 1M
sector: Technology
exchange: NASDAQ
above_sma: 50
above_sma: 200
sma_rising: 200
near_52w_high: 25%
limit: 50
[/screen]

[b]Risk[/b]
Position size is fixed at 1R. See [url=https://example.com/risk]the risk notes[/url].

[pine]
//@version=5
strategy("Minervini", overlay=true)
sma200 = ta.sma(close, 200)
plot(sma200)
if close > sma200 and close[1] <= sma200
    strategy.entry("L", strategy.long)
[/pine]
