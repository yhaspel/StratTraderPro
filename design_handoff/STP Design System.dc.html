12 — Principles & accessibility
Semantics are sacred. Green/red mean money and risk only. Steel means "the machine is working" (actions, focus, progress, equity). Amber means degraded or attention. Every status carries a text label — color is reinforcement, never the message. Honest states. Unavailable features render disabled with a reason (TradeStation, LIVE mode). MFA-gated data says so instead of "no data". Destructive actions always confirm with MFA, in one consistent pattern.
WCAG AA. Semantic text pairs use deep steps (up #2E6B45, down #A63D36 ≥ 4.5:1 on the ground); accent for body-size text uses accent-700 per Industry's own rule. Focus = 2px accent ring. Keep existing patterns: role=alert, aria-busy, focus-trapped dialogs, skip link, 44px mobile targets. Blueprint grammar. Panels are line drawings with registration marks; the solid accent primary button is the one filled object. No decorative color beyond steel + the semantic extension. Motion: 120ms hovers, 200ms overlays, nothing decorative near money.
